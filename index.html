The problem

People know.
They just don't act.

Awareness isn't the bottleneck. The missing piece is a personal, immediate, social reason to care today — not in 2050. We use behavioural science to bridge that gap.

i.

Existing apps need manual input

Nobody logs their commute every morning. Self-reported data is unreliable, tedious, and gameable.

ii.

Google Maps already does eco-routing

Competing on routing is pointless. We track your whole lifestyle — travel, food, electricity, screen time.

iii.

No financial or social incentive

Environmental guilt doesn't change behaviour. Money and peer pressure do. We use both.

Data integrity

Fraud-proof
by design.

Permissions are optional — but only verified data unlocks coupons. Users choose their level of trust.

GPS + fuel SMS

Verified

GPS confirms movement. Fuel SMS from HPCL/IOCL confirms actual vehicle use — more accurate than distance-based estimates.

Food order SMS

Verified

Swiggy/Zomato SMS parsed on-device. Items keyword-matched to veg/non-veg. No user input, no way to fake it.

Screen time API

Verified

Android Digital Wellbeing — OS-level data, tamper-proof. Converted to server energy emissions automatically.

UPI + merchant SMS

Partial

Bank SMS parsed locally. High-carbon merchants auto-flagged. Unclassified transactions get a small flat-rate score.

Electricity — DISCOM

Verified

Government API fetch via consumer number. Monthly units pulled directly — no user-reported estimates.

On-device privacy

Privacy-first

All SMS parsing happens locally. Raw SMS never leaves your phone. Only the calculated emission value hits our server.

The why
"People don't lack awareness — they lack a personal, social, and financial reason to act on it today."
i.

Social pressure works

A named leaderboard within your college creates real peer accountability — not abstract global guilt.

ii.

Financial framing converts

"Metro twice a week saves ₹600/month" lands harder than any CO₂ statistic. We frame in rupees first.

iii.

Identity drives sharing

A weekly Carbon Wrapped — shareable like Spotify Wrapped — turns your footprint into something people post.

iv.

Institutional potential

Flip the target to colleges or companies — a campus carbon audit is a natural B2B extension.
